# PMBC Phase History

Every phase of the build, in the order the rows were written, with the reasoning
and the things that went wrong. Split out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-08-13: that
file is loaded into context at the start of every session, and this table had
grown to 75KB of it, which is a cost paid on every session to carry detail that
matters on perhaps one.

**Nothing here was edited on the way across.** The rows are as they were written
at the time, so a claim about what was true in June stays a claim about June.

Read this when you need to know **why** something is the way it is. For the
current state of the project, read `CLAUDE.md`. For the chronological account
of a working session, including what was tried and abandoned, read
`SESSION_LOG.md`.
